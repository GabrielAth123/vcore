Why VCORE

A real registered office, not just an address on paper.

With VCORE, your registered address is backed by a real lease: we issue a lease for a defined space in our office, our accountant files it electronically with AADE, the Greek tax authority, and your business name can go on a sign at the office.

You can use it to register your company with GEMI (the Greek business registry), the tax office and EFKA, for VIES if you trade within the EU, and with Greek banks, always subject to each authority’s and bank’s own requirements and to your business activity being eligible.

We receive your mail and parcels, let you know when they arrive, and scan them or forward them to you abroad. When you are in Greece, you can use our office for meetings by arrangement.

How it works

From first contact to an active registered address, you can complete the process without coming to Greece.

01 / 05

Get in touch

Tell us about your business: a new Greek company, a branch of your foreign company, or an existing business moving its address. By email, phone or WhatsApp, in English.

02 / 05

Activity check

We check with the accountant that your business activity (KAD code) can be registered at a virtual office address. Services, e-shops and trading without a warehouse are a good fit; a physical shop, restaurant or warehouse is not.

03 / 05

Sign the agreement

You sign the Service Agreement, remotely with a qualified electronic signature if you are outside Greece, and we issue the lease for your registered address.

04 / 05

We file the lease

Our accountant files the lease electronically with AADE, the Greek tax authority.

05 / 05

You receive the lease

We send you the lease, usually within 24 to 48 hours of receiving your documents, ready for your company registration or address change.
