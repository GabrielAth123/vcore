KAVALA

Receive in Greece.
Forward anywhere.

A professional parcel receiving address in Kavala, Greece. We receive your parcels, hold them temporarily, and coordinate forwarding to your preferred destination in Turkey, the Balkans, the Middle East or across the EU.

How It Works

One address in Kavala. Four steps to wherever you are.

  1. 01

    Give us as your delivery address

    Use our Kavala address at checkout, with a supplier, or with any marketplace that needs a Greek delivery point.

  2. 02

    We receive it and notify you

    Your parcel arrives at our facility. We check it in and let you know it's here, the same day.

  3. 03

    We hold it securely

    CCTV-monitored storage with humidity control, for as long as you need before deciding what's next.

  4. 04

    You collect it, or we forward it

    Pick it up in person, or tell us where to send it. We coordinate the forwarding with local shipping partners.

Real-World Examples

One receiving point. Multiple destinations.

Turkey

The most common route. Customers and businesses in Turkey use our Kavala address to shop across Greek stores, consolidate multiple orders into one shipment, or gather supplier samples before committing to a bulk order.

Balkans & EU

For shoppers across Europe buying from Greek stores that don't ship internationally, businesses sourcing from European suppliers, and sellers who need a local Greek returns address for their EU customers.

Middle East & Egypt

Customers across the Middle East and Egypt use Kavala as their gateway to Greek and European online stores that would otherwise be out of reach.

Also handled here: spare parts and equipment for boat owners cruising the islands, waiting at the port before they arrive.

Additional Notes
Terms & Liability
  • Prohibited items (drugs, anything illegal to send or possess) are never accepted, under any circumstances.
  • VCORE is not a courier. Loss, damage or delay caused by a carrier is the carrier's responsibility, not ours.
  • Parcels are held securely in Kavala (CCTV, monitored storage) until collected or forwarded.
  • Customs or police may inspect any parcel at any time; we fully cooperate.
  • We never open a sealed parcel, except when legally required to.

Prohibited items. Narcotics, illegal drugs, and any other item that is illegal to send, receive, or possess under Greek, EU, or destination-country law may not be sent to our address under any circumstances. Sending or attempting to send prohibited items is entirely at the sender's and receiver's own risk and responsibility, and VCORE accepts no liability arising from it.

Courier and seller responsibility. VCORE is not a courier, shipping, or logistics company. We are not liable for damage, loss, or delay caused by the courier, postal service, or shipping partner handling a parcel before it reaches our facility or after it leaves it, nor for issues caused by the store, marketplace, or seller a parcel was purchased from. Any claim relating to damage, loss, or delay caused by a shipping carrier should be directed to that carrier.

Storage. Parcels are held at our facility in Kavala, which is secured and monitored by CCTV cameras, with humidity and storage conditions monitored, for as long as reasonably necessary before collection or onward forwarding.

Inspection by authorities. Parcels may be subject to inspection by customs authorities or the police at any point, including inspection by police narcotics-detection dogs. VCORE fully cooperates with any such inspection and has no control over its outcome or any resulting delay.

Sealed handling. We receive parcels sealed and closed, and we forward or hand them over sealed and closed. VCORE does not open, inspect, or tamper with the contents of any parcel at any stage, except where legally required to do so by customs or law enforcement authorities.
