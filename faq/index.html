Virtual Office & Business Registration

What is a Virtual Office in Greece and how does it work?

VCORE's Virtual Office provides a professional business address in Greece without the need to rent physical office space, allowing full remote management of your business operations.

Can I use a Virtual Office to register my company with GEMI?

Yes. VCORE provides all the necessary documents so that the address can be used as the registered seat of your company for registration with the General Commercial Registry (GEMI).

Is using a Virtual Office legal in Greece?

Absolutely. The use of virtual office services is a legal and widely established business practice under Greek law.

What types of businesses can use the Virtual Office?

The service covers a range of corporate structures including IKE, EPE, AE and sole traders, provided their business activity permits the use of a virtual registered address.

Can any business activity use the registered address?

Not all. Before signing the lease, both VCORE and your accountant check your business activity code (ΚΑΔ). The address suits services, e-shops and trading without a warehouse. It does not suit a physical shop, food service or storage of goods.

Can I use the Virtual Office for an IKE company?

Yes. The IKE (Private Capital Company) is one of the most common corporate structures that use VCORE's virtual office services as their registered seat.

Do I need to rent a physical office to incorporate or operate my company?

No. As long as your business activity permits the use of a virtual address, no physical office rental is required for either registration or day-to-day operation.

Can I transfer my existing registered address to VCORE?

Yes. VCORE can support the full process of transferring your company's existing registered address to our location, in accordance with all required legal procedures.

Do you provide the necessary documents for GEMI registration?

Yes. All required documents and supporting materials for registration or amendment of your registered address at GEMI are provided.

Can multiple companies use the same address?

Yes. Multiple independent businesses can legally use the same professional address in accordance with the applicable Greek legal framework.

Is the Virtual Office suitable for startups and online businesses?

Yes. The Virtual Office is an ideal solution for startups, freelancers and online businesses looking for a professional presence at significantly lower operational cost.

How quickly is the Virtual Office activated?

Activation typically takes place within 24 to 48 hours of receiving the required documentation.

Can I use VCORE's address on Google Business Profile?

Yes. The professional address can be used to strengthen your business's online presence, in accordance with Google's policies.
