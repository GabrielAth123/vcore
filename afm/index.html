Greek Tax Number (AFM) for Foreigners & Foreign Companies

Need a Greek tax number while living abroad? VCORE Business Services in Kavala, Greece, assists non-residents and foreign companies with Greek tax registration, document preparation and authorised representation where required.

An AFM is a nine digit Greek tax identification number, also called the Greek TIN or Greek taxpayer identification number. You may need one for a property purchase, inheritance or participation in a Greek company.

Whether you are buying property in Greece, handling an inheritance, becoming a shareholder or completing a business transaction, we help coordinate the process. Many applications can be coordinated remotely. We will confirm the procedure for your circumstances.

Personal AFM

Personal Greek AFM for Non-Residents

If you live outside Greece, you may need a personal Greek tax identification number (AFM) for a property purchase, inheritance, participation in a Greek company or another transaction involving Greek tax obligations.

What Is an AFM in Greece?

An AFM is the nine digit Greek tax identification number, also called the Greek TIN or Greek taxpayer identification number. It identifies the taxpayer in the Greek tax system.

You may need one for a property purchase, inheritance or participation in a Greek company.

Can I Get a Greek AFM Without Visiting Greece?

Yes, remote procedures are available. VCORE can coordinate your application through an appropriately authorised person, depending on your circumstances and the requirements of the Greek tax authority (AADE).

Where an authorisation is required, we prepare the relevant Greek text and explain the signing procedure. Depending on the document and country, this may involve a Greek consulate, a notary, an Apostille or another accepted form of certification.

Before you arrange certifications or send original documents, we explain what is required for your particular application.

Can I Apply Directly Through AADE and Book an Appointment?

AADE also provides an online application procedure for individuals, including identity verification through its myAADElive video appointment service. Not every applicant needs to appoint a representative to submit the application.

VCORE's service is intended for clients who prefer professional assistance with the documentation, authorisation and coordination of the procedure.

What Is a Kleidarithmos?

Your AFM is your Greek tax identification number. The authentication key (kleidarithmos) is used to activate access to AADE's online tax services, such as TAXISnet and myAADE. They serve different purposes.

For individuals applying for a new AFM, the electronic registration procedure also includes a request for an authentication key.

Company AFM

Greek AFM for Foreign Companies

A company already incorporated outside Greece may need a Greek tax identification number for certain transactions or obligations in Greece.

A foreign company may need a Greek AFM, for example, when it purchases or owns Greek property or participates in a Greek company. The applicable registration requirements depend on the company's activities and legal position.

Does My Foreign Company Need to Establish a Greek Company?

Not necessarily. In certain circumstances, an existing foreign company can obtain a Greek AFM without incorporating a separate Greek company.

However, obtaining an AFM does not by itself determine whether a branch, VAT registration or other tax obligations are required. These matters must be assessed separately.

How Does VCORE Assist?

We first establish where the company is incorporated, the intended transaction in Greece and who is authorised to represent the company.

Our in-house accountant reviews the appropriate tax registration procedure. We then coordinate the required documentation, authorisations and application steps.

Before you order foreign company certificates, arrange an Apostille or pay for translations, we explain which documents are required for your case.

Required Documents

What Documents Are Required for a Greek AFM?

The supporting documents depend on whether the applicant is an individual or a legal entity, the country involved and the procedure used.

VCORE assists clients from the EU, UK, US and other countries. Requirements depend on the applicant and country.

For Individuals

We will usually begin by requesting the following information:

  • A power of attorney, signed at a Greek embassy or consulate, or before a notary with an Apostille. We prepare the Greek text for you.
  • The original power of attorney, sent to us by post.
  • A colour scan of your passport.
  • A passport photo.
  • Your tax identification number in your country of residence.
  • Your home address and contact details (email and mobile phone).
  • If you are married, your marriage certificate with an official translation, where required.

Foreign public documents need an Apostille, or a consular legalisation for countries outside the Hague Convention, before they are officially translated into Greek.

For Foreign Companies

Depending on the application, the documentation may include:

  • Company incorporation or registration documents.
  • Evidence of the company's current legal existence.
  • Documents identifying its legal representative.
  • Authorisation for the person handling the application, where required.
  • Supporting information about the intended transaction in Greece.
  • Official translations and document legalisation, where required.

Important: Do not arrange notarisation, an Apostille or certified translations before we confirm the requirements for your application.

We will also explain which documents can be sent electronically and whether any originals must be delivered.

After You Receive Your Greek AFM

If you buy property in Greece, you declare it on the E9 form by 31 March of the year after the purchase, and you pay the annual property tax (ENFIA). Both are done online on myAADE with your access codes.

You file a Greek income tax return only if you have income from Greece, for example rent.

Common Questions

Common Questions About Greek Tax Numbers

Does Getting a Greek AFM Make Me a Greek Tax Resident?

No. Obtaining a Greek tax identification number does not, by itself, make you a Greek tax resident. Tax residence is determined under separate legal criteria.

However, transactions, income or assets in Greece may create Greek tax obligations even if you remain a tax resident of another country.

Do I Need a Tax Representative in Greece?

The appointment of a tax representative is not mandatory in every case. The applicable rules depend on the taxpayer's circumstances and the arrangements for receiving communications from the Greek tax authority.

An authorised person handling an AFM application and a formally appointed tax representative are not necessarily the same thing.

Tell us your circumstances and we will explain whether a tax representative is required for your application.

Where a tax representative is required, VCORE takes on this role through our in-house accountant.

Is a Greek AFM the Same as a VAT Registration?

No. An AFM identifies the taxpayer in the Greek tax system. Whether a business must register for VAT or comply with additional tax obligations depends on its activities and circumstances.

Can I Get a Greek Tax Number (AFM) if I Do Not Work in Greece?

Yes. You do not need to work or live in Greece to get an AFM. Any foreigner can get one, for example to buy property or a car, open a bank account or handle an inheritance. VCORE handles it remotely through a power of attorney, so you do not need to travel to Greece.
